SUPPORT & RESOURCES

Support, with care.

The Unsaid offers a calm space for listening, reflection, emotional support and personal guidance. If you need immediate help or specialist medical or mental-health care, the resources below can connect you with trained services.

When urgent support is needed

If you or someone nearby may be in immediate danger or needs urgent medical or psychiatric attention, contact emergency services or a qualified professional straight away.

Support in India

Immediate danger or emergency: Dial 112, India’s pan-India emergency number.

Mental-health support: Call Tele-MANAS at 14416 or 1800-89-14416. It offers government-supported tele-mental-health assistance. For immediate danger, contact emergency services on 112.

Official information: Emergency Response Support System · National Mental Health Programme / Tele-MANAS.

What conversations can cover

Relationship problems, loneliness, heartbreak, overthinking, family pressure, career confusion, self-doubt, life decisions and other personal concerns.

What to expect

Every session is shaped around the concern you bring. We focus on listening, reflection and practical next steps; each person’s experience is different. For cancellations and refunds, see the dedicated Cancellation & Refunds page.
