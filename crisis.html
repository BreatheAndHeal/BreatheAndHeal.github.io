IMPORTANT

Support has boundaries.

A Little Lighter is positioned as a space for listening, reflection, emotional support and personal guidance. It is not an emergency service and should not be presented as a replacement for qualified medical or mental-health care.

Urgent situations

If someone may be in immediate danger, is at risk of self-harm, or needs urgent medical or psychiatric attention, contact local emergency services or a qualified professional immediately.

Scope

Conversations can cover relationship problems, loneliness, heartbreak, overthinking, family pressure, career confusion, self-doubt and other personal concerns. Avoid diagnosing conditions or promising guaranteed healing.

Before launch

Add the exact safeguarding, consent, refund/cancellation, privacy and professional-boundary information that matches the service you actually provide.
