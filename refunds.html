CANCELLATION & REFUNDS

A straightforward approach to changes.

Because sessions reserve a specific time, customers should contact THE UNSAID as early as possible when they need to cancel or reschedule.

Before the session

Cancellation or rescheduling requests received before the scheduled start time can be reviewed for a refund or a new slot, depending on availability and the circumstances of the request.

Late cancellation or no-show

When a customer cancels very close to the session or does not attend, a refund may not be available because the reserved appointment time may not be reusable. Contact us promptly so the circumstances can be reviewed.

If THE UNSAID cancels

If THE UNSAID cannot conduct a confirmed session, the customer can request either a rescheduled appointment or a refund through the original payment route.

Payment failures

If a payment fails and the payment provider reports that funds were actually debited, customers should wait for the payment status/reversal or contact support. Do not make a second payment until the first transaction is clearly failed or reversed.

How to request help

Send the Booking ID, scheduled date/time and email used for the booking. This helps locate the reservation and payment record quickly.
