THE IDEA BEHIND THE SPACE

“Sometimes you don't need another lecture. You need somewhere to finally say what you've been carrying.”

A Little Lighter is built around a simple belief: a difficult moment can feel very different when you don't have to hold it alone for a while.

This is a private conversation space for listening, reflection, emotional support and personal perspective. The goal is not to tell you how to live your life. It is to create enough room for you to hear yourself more clearly.

Important: This service is not a substitute for emergency help, diagnosis, psychotherapy or medical treatment. Please use qualified professional care when your situation requires it.

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

Is this therapy?

This site is positioned as emotional support, listening and personal guidance. It does not present itself as a replacement for licensed psychotherapy, psychiatric care or medical treatment.

Which language can I use?

Hindi, Hinglish and English are supported.

How does the online session happen?

The booking request is confirmed first. A WhatsApp or Google Meet link can then be shared for the agreed time.

Can I talk about anything?

You can bring relationship, loneliness, overthinking, family, career, self-doubt, life decisions and other personal situations. Safety or medical emergencies need appropriate professional or emergency services.
