“You don't always need someone to fix your life. Sometimes you need enough room to hear yourself again.”

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

A few useful details before you choose a session.

What can I talk about in a session?

You can bring overthinking, relationship concerns, heartbreak, loneliness, family pressure, career confusion, self-doubt, life decisions or something else you would like to put into words.

How do online sessions work? Do I need my camera on?

After your payment is verified and the booking is confirmed, your session details and Google Meet link are sent to your booking email. You can keep your camera off; video is not required. Prefer a regular phone call? Contact us before booking so we can confirm whether it can be arranged.

How should I prepare for my first session?

You do not need a perfect explanation. A few honest words about what is on your mind are enough. You can write down one or two things you would most like to explore, and share only what you feel comfortable sharing.

What session lengths and languages are available?

Emotional Guidance is available in 20, 40 and 60-minute sessions. Vedic Astrology readings are available in 20, 40 and 60-minute formats. You can choose Hindi, Hinglish or English for both Emotional Guidance and Vedic Astrology.

What should I know before booking a Vedic astrology reading?

Vedic Astrology is offered as a traditional practice for guidance and reflection. Your reading uses the birth details supplied and focuses on the area you choose, helping you explore chart themes and possible perspectives. For significant medical, legal or financial decisions, seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

How is my information handled?

The form asks for information needed to arrange and manage your booking. For details about booking information, payment and scheduling providers, read the Privacy page.

What if I need to cancel or reschedule?

If you cancel at least 24 hours before your session, you may choose a full refund to your original payment method or one free reschedule, subject to availability. For cancellations less than 24 hours before the session, a refund is normally not available; at our discretion, we may offer one reschedule, subject to availability. No-shows are not refundable or reschedulable. If The Unsaid cancels, you may choose a full refund or a free reschedule. Approved refunds usually appear in 5–7 working days after initiation. Read the full Cancellation & Refunds policy.

What if money is debited but my booking status is unclear?

Please do not make a second payment. Contact us with your Booking ID, scheduled date and time, the email address used for the booking, and your payment reference if available. Within 48 hours of receiving the details needed to investigate, we will confirm the booking or initiate a refund if we cannot confirm it. A refund usually appears in the original payment method within 5–7 working days after initiation.
