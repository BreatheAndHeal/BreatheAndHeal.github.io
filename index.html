01

QUESTIONS

Before you book.

What can I talk about in a session?

You can bring relationship concerns, overthinking, loneliness, family pressure, career confusion, self-doubt, life decisions or simply something you have been carrying and want to put into words.

How should I prepare for my first session?

You do not need a perfect explanation. A few honest words about what is on your mind are enough to begin. You can also write down one or two things you most want clarity on.

How long does a session last?

You can choose a 30, 45 or 60-minute emotional guidance session. Astrology readings are available in 30, 50 and 70-minute formats.

Which language can I use?

Hindi, Hinglish and English are supported. Choose the language that feels most natural when you book.

What happens after I book?

Choose an available time, complete the secure payment, and your booking is confirmed. A Google Calendar event and Google Meet link are created automatically, and booking details are sent to the email you provide.

How is my information handled?

Only the information needed to arrange and communicate about your booking is requested. See the Privacy page for details about the booking data, payment and scheduling tools used by the service.

Can I book a Vedic astrology session separately?

Yes. Choose Vedic Astrology in the booking section, select the reading you want, and provide the birth details needed for the requested reading.
