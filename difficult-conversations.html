DIFFICULT CONVERSATIONS

How to Say the Thing You've Been Avoiding

Honest conversations rarely come with perfect wording. The goal is to be clear, respectful and real.

Start with what is true for you

Use simple first-person language: “I've been feeling…”, “I need…”, “I want to understand…” This can make a difficult message easier to hear.

Say the important part early

Long explanations can hide the sentence that actually matters. Decide the one thing you most need the other person to know, and say it plainly.

Leave room for their answer

A conversation has two sides. You can state your need or boundary clearly while still allowing the other person to respond in their own words.
