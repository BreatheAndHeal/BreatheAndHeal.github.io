PRIVACY

Your information, handled with care.

This privacy notice describes the information the current THE UNSAID booking flow collects and how it is used. It is written for the current website and tools and should be reviewed when the service or its data practices change.

Information we collect

For a booking, the site may collect your name, email address, phone number, selected service, date and time, language, and a short message or concern. For astrology bookings, it may also collect birth date, exact birth time and birth place because those details are part of the requested reading.

How it is used

Booking information is used to create and manage your appointment, verify payment, create the scheduled Google Calendar/Google Meet event, send booking-related notifications, and communicate about the service.

Service providers

The current booking system uses Razorpay for payment processing, Neon for booking data storage, Vercel for server-side booking functions, GitHub Pages for the public website, Google Calendar/Google Meet plus Google Apps Script for scheduling and notifications, and Open-Meteo for worldwide birth-place search suggestions. Booking messages and astrology birth details are stored in the booking database and are sent to the scheduling integration only as needed to administer the appointment. The calendar event does not intentionally include the customer’s private message or concern. When you use the birth-place search, the text you type may be sent to Open-Meteo’s geocoding service to return matching places. Each provider has its own terms and privacy practices.

Information in your messages

Please share only information you are comfortable providing. Do not submit payment-card details, passwords, government ID numbers or other information that is not necessary for the service.

Retention and deletion

Booking information is retained only as reasonably necessary for appointment administration, payment reconciliation, safety, dispute handling and applicable legal obligations. Questions or requests about your booking information can be sent to the contact below.

Security

Payment credentials are handled by the payment provider rather than stored in the public website. Server-side credentials and API secrets are kept outside the public HTML. Booking-status access is additionally protected by a per-booking access token, payment verification uses server-side Razorpay signature and API checks, and booking endpoints apply request-rate limits. No online system can guarantee absolute security.
