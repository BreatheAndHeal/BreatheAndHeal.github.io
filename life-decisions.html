LIFE DECISIONS

How to Think Clearly About a Big Life Decision

When a decision matters deeply, the mind can turn every possibility into a threat. A calmer framework can make the choice easier to examine.

Write the decision in one sentence

Replace “What should I do with my life?” with a concrete question such as “Which of these two options fits my next six months better?” Precision reduces noise.

Separate facts from fears

Make three columns: what I know, what I suspect, and what I fear. Keeping them separate prevents every imagined outcome from feeling equally real.

Choose the trade-off you can live with

There may be no option with zero discomfort. Ask which costs, risks and compromises fit your values and actual circumstances better.
